### Shared art direction and restrictions

Premium cinematic anime-cyberpunk London 2035, original characters and motifs, subtle high-tech steampunk material details. Base `#05070D`; VOLT `#22E4FF`; FLARE `#FFB020`; ICE `#B48CFF`; ZERO DAY `#FF2E5B`. Glass, brushed titanium, tasteful haze and crisp rim light. No words, numbers, fake glyphs, signage, logos, famous characters or people, weapons or gore in any generated sample. **The exact CYBER CYPHER wordmark is the only text exception.**

**Emblem:** Bold distinctive cyan hexagon crest readable at 64 px, carefully articulated edges, isolated for alpha extraction.

**HALO:** Luminous white-cyan concentric torus, soft abstract face-like radiance, unmistakably nonhuman, circle-crop safe.

**Title:** Rainy future-London rooftop panorama, cinematic architecture and subtle holographic colours, dark calm central 70% behind title/menu, no readable signs.

**Node:** Dark smoked glass and fine peripheral circuit traces, featureless central 60% with white text contrast 7:1, no owner-coded decoration.

**Static:** Original adult three-quarter-view head-and-shoulders cyberpunk anime agent, angular silver spiked hair, segmented visor, dark jacket, cyan rim lighting, friendly confident expression, square 256 px composition.
